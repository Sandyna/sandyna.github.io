Depending on what you buy, you can accomodate various dietary restrictions. There are gluten-free tortillas, diary-free mayo, meat substitutes... Recipe is listed in its original version, but feel free to replace anything. <br/>
It's also an excellent way of finishing random things lying around in the fridge. A bit of cheese, unfinished can of beans, three leftover mushrooms, they can all go in. If you buy a head of lettuce and a bag of tomatoes, this meal can be eaten for a few days, until you run out. <br/>

## Ingredients
* 1 pack tortillas (!)
* 4-5 chicken nuggies (!)
* salad
* tomatoes
* mayo (!)

## Optional Ingredients
* Avocado
* Any vegetable you like, fresh or canned
* Roasted mushrooms
* Sour cream, yoghurt (!)
* Oregano, dill or garlic powder
* Cheese, grated or sliced (!)

## Instructions
Bake chicken nuggies according to instructions. <br/>
Prepare veggies while the nuggies are baking. Wash, cut, do whatever you do. <br/>
Microwave tortilla for 30s if you can be bothered. Spread mayo in a stripe in the middle. Apply any herbs or seasoning on it. <br/>
Put nuggies on the mayo. Put veggies on the nuggies. Wrap. Eat. <br/>
