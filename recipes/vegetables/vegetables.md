There is two ways to prepare vegetables so that they do not become a mushy mess. They either need to be done just enough to begin cooking, but still keep the crispiness from their raw state, or they need to be cooked so long they get crispy from browning in the pan. 
When using frozen vegetables, it's a bit tricky to get the first state, so I recommend going for the second.
