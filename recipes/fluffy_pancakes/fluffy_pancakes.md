## Ingredients
* 300 g all-purpose or plain flour
* 60 g granulated sugar
* 18 g baking powder
* 0.25 teaspoon baking soda
* 3 g salt
* 440 ml milk
* 60 g butter, melted and cooled
* 10 ml pure vanilla extract
* 1 large egg

## Instructions
Combine together the dry ingredients. Make a well in the centre and add the wet ingredients. <br/>
Whisk the wet ingredients together first before slowly folding them into the dry ingredients. Mix together until smooth (there may be a couple of lumps but that's okay).<br/>
The batter will be thick. If you find the batter too thick -- doesn't pour off the ladle or out of the measuring cup smoothly -- fold a couple tablespoons of extra milk into the batter at a time until reaching desired consistency).<br/>
Set the batter aside and allow to rest while heating up your pan or griddle.<br/>
Heat a nonstick pan or griddle over low-medium heat and wipe over with a little butter to lightly grease pan. Pour batter onto the pan and spread out gently.<br/>
When the underside is golden and bubbles begin to appear on the surface, flip and cook until golden.<br/>
Serve with honey, maple syrup, fruit, ice cream or frozen yoghurt, or enjoy plain!<br/>
Note: to get the pancakes nice and round, make them in a small pan. <br/>
