## Ingredients
* 250g Ground pork
* 20g chives, finely chopped
* 2 cloves Garlic, minced
* 1⁄2 inch Ginger, minced
* 1⁄4 Cabbage thinly sliced and diced
* 1⁄4 tsp pepper
* 1⁄2 tsp Salt
* 1⁄2 - 3⁄4 tsp Baking soda
* 2 tsp Sesame oil
* 2 Tbsp Soy sauce
* 2 Tbsp Water
* 2-3 Tbsp Cornstarch
* Sheets Gyoza wrappers

## Instructions
1. Mix pork, chives together and fold in the diced cabbage.<br/>
2. Stir in the remaining ingredients and add baking soda. Loosen up the 
mixture with the water so it becomes nice and juicy.<br/>
3. To form the dumplings, remove 1 wonton wrapper from the package,
covering the others with a damp cloth. Brush the edges of the wrapper
lightly with water. Place 1⁄2 rounded teaspoon of the mixture in the center
of the wrapper. Shape as desired. Set on a sheet pan and cover with a
damp cloth. Repeat procedure until all of the filling is gone.<br/>
4. Place a medium nonstick skillet on stove top, medium heat. Add oil to
bottom of pan. Place dumplings in pan, let the bottom get crispy. Pour
about 2 tablespoons water into pan. Place cover on top of the pan. Let
cook until water evaporates. Plate and serve. <br/>
