## Ingredients
* 600g sweet potatoes
* cut into 2-3cm chunks
* 100g rice, cooked and cooled
* 3 spring onions, finely sliced
* 60g dried cranberries
* dill
* parsley, finely chopped
* 2 eggs, beaten
* 1 lemon, zested and juiced
* 2 pcs puff pastry, 
* flour, for dusting
* 150g soft goat cheese
* 2 tbsp seeds (sesame, poppy, linseed, or a mixture)
* lingonberry or cranberry sauce
* soured cream, to serve

## Instructions
Heat the oven to 200C/180C fan/gas 6. Put the sweet potatoes and oil on a large baking tray with some seasoning and toss. Roast for 20-30 mins until golden and tender. Leave to cool.<br/>
Tip the rice into a bowl with the spring onions, cranberries, dill, parsley, lemon zest and half the lemon juice. Add half the beaten egg, then mix well. Tip in the cooled sweet potatoes and stir gently so you don’t break up the potato too much.<br/>
Combine the goat cheese with the remaining lemon juice to loosen, then spread over the pastry, leaving a 2cm border. Spoon the rice filling on top, and press down firmly to make a smooth shape. Put the second pastry on and press the edges together. Trim the edges, then crimp or press with a fork to seal. Brush all over with the remaining beaten egg, then score a pattern on if you like using a sharp knife, and sprinkle over the seeds. Chill for at least 30 mins, or up to 24 hrs.<br/>
Heat the oven to 200C/180C fan/gas 6. Bake the coulibiac for 40 mins until golden and crisp. Serve in thick slices, with dollops of lingonberry or cranberry sauce and soured cream.<br/>
Notes: Recipe starts with a block of pastry that gets rolled out to 15x45cm and a second one to cover it. Just buy one puff pastry sheets and fold it in half, or buy two and make a little more filling, or cut off the excess and use for something else. <br/>
