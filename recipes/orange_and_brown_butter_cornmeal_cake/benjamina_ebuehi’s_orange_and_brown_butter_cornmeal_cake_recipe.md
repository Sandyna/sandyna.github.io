The cake is rather plain, but still tasty. The stars of the show are the grainy texture from the cornmeal and the citrus. I tried it with lime zest instead of orange. I think you might get away with not browning the butter if you want to make it easier on yourself. 

## Ingredients
* 120g unsalted butter
* 125g sugar
* Zest of 2 large oranges
* 2 large eggs
* 80ml milk
* 25-30g (2 tbsp) neutral oil (vegetable or sunflower)
* 100g plain flour
* 75g fine cornmeal
* 1 tsp baking powder
* ¼ tsp bicarbonate of soda
* ¼ tsp salt
* Icing sugar, to dust

## Instructions
Grease and line a 20cm round cake tin. You can also dust it with cornmeal. Heat the oven to 180C (160C fan)/350F/gas 4.<br/>
Brown the butter: Melt the butter in a small saucepan over a medium heat, then let it cook, stirring occasionally, until it stops sizzling and starts to smell nutty and the milk solids have turned brown. Remove from the heat and pour into a bowl to cool.<br/>
Put the sugar and orange zest in a blender, blend until it resembles wet sand. In a bowl whisk the eggs, milk and oil, add sugar and mix to combine.<br/>
In a separate bowl, mix the flour, cornmeal, baking powder, bicarb and salt. Sift into the wet ingredients, mix until smooth. Stir in the cooled brown butter, then pour the batter into the prepared tin.<br/>
Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until a skewer/knife inserted into the centre comes out clean. Leave the cake to cool completely before dusting with a little icing sugar and serving. (I won't, lol)<br/>
