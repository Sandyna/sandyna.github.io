## Ingredients
* 400 g mixture of frozen sea food (scallops, shrimp, squid)
* 600 g frozen salmon and alaska pollock
* 250 g white onion (one big)
* 1 red bell pepper
* 1 green bell pepper
* 1 baby bok choy
* 2 carrots
* 1 bulb of garlic
* 1 tbl sp mild oil
* 800 g diced tomatoes (fresh or canned)
* 0.5 l chicken broth or seafood broth; I used lobster stock 
* 2 dl heavy cream
* salt and herbs to taste – I used oregano, basil, thyme, 4 bay leaves

## Instructions
1. Preheat oil on medium heat. Add peppers and onions and cook until
   tender. About 3-5 minutes.<br/>
2. Add carrots and garlic. Cook for an additional 2 minutes.<br/>
3. Add in tomatoes, tomato sauce, broth and seasonings. Simmer covered on
   low 30 minutes.<br/>
4. Add the frozen fish. Boil covered for 5 minutes.<br/>
5. Add sea food, and baby bok choy. Boil covered for an additional 3-5
   minutes.<br/>
6. Pour in the cream, stir it in.<br/>
Garnish with fresh parsley, serve with freshly toasted garlic bread.<br/>
