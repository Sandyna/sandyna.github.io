## Ingredients
* Chocolate
* Heavy cream

## Instructions
Instructions are roughly the same for any type. Chop chocolate, heat cream, pour cream over chocolate to melt it, let cool. What differs are the ratios of chocolate and cream. <br/>

## Ratios

| Chocolate type | Chocolate:Cream | Use      |
|----------------|-----------------|----------|
| White          | 2.5:1           | truffles |
| White          | x:x             | whipped  |
| White          | x:x             | spread   |
| White          | x:x             | pour     |
| Milk           | x:x             | truffles |
| Milk           | x:x             | whipped  |
| Milk           | x:x             | spread   |
| Milk           | x:x             | pour     |
| Dark           | x:x             | truffles |
| Dark           | x:x             | whipped  |
| Dark           | x:x             | spread   |
| Dark           | x:x             | pour     |

## Examples
Lavender white chocolate truffles: 250g white chocolate, 100g cream, 2g lavender. Let lavender steep in the cream as it heats up. Let cool in the fridge, roll into balls quickly and coat in glitter sugar. Stick back into fridge if it starts to stick to hands. <br.>
