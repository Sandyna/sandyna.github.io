## Ingredients
* 1 Camembert
* 3-5 thin slices Bacon
* Black pepper, ground
* Cinnamon, ground
* Maple syrup

## Instructions
Preheat the oven to 180°C. Wrap camembert in bacon. Spices can be applied on the cheese or on the bacon. Chef's choice. <br/>
Bake 10-15min. Towards the end, pour a dash of maple sirup on the bacon and bake a few minutes more. <br/>
Serve with a salat, veggies, bread, or nothing at all if you can't be arsed. <br/>
