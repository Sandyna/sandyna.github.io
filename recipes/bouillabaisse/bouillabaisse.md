## Ingredients
* 3 kinds of fish, ~400g of each (for example bream, tuna, salmon, cod, shrimps...)
* 200g butter
* 10 tomatoes or one tomato can (between 400g and 800g)
* 350ml white wine (optional)
* 10 shallots
* 2 carrots
* 2 apples (optional)
* 1/2 leek
* 1 branch celery
* 3 fresh fennels
* 5 cloves of garlic
* Aromatics
  * saffron (important)
  * thyme 
  * parsley
  * lemon peel
  * orange peel
  * bay leaf
  * black pepper
  * salt to taste
* orange juice from 3 oranges (optional)
* 3l stock, preferably fish stock

## Instructions
Cut all the vegetables into soup-sized chunks. Roast them slightly in butter to get the nice aroma going. You can add things gradually as you cut them. Once mostly heated, add aromatics except saffron. Add wine and stock and cook for roughly 10 minutes.<br/>
Add tomatoes or tomato can and saffron and bring to boil. Cut fish into similarly sized pieces and add to the soup. Cook until the fish is just done but not overcooked. Salt and pepper to taste, optionally squeeze in some orange juice (not the carton stuff!) and remove from heat.<br/>
Serve and pimp up as you wish.<br/>
Note: The cooking time is quite short for an authentic Bouillabaisse to leave the ingredients with some of their characteristic tastes. Saffron is non-negotiable.<br/>
Some pimpage options: 
- Parsley on top
- Lightly butter-roasted orange peel on top
- Baguette
- Toast with mayo, garlic, chili and saffron
- Blend the soup up
- Add blended stock veggies to thicken it up
