Frosting is a cream that goes on top of cakes, cupcakes, etc. Thicker than icing or glaze, it's usually applied with a piping tool or spread with a spatula. 

## Frosting types - Buttercream

| Name                 | Description                                                                | Ingredients                           | Difficulty |
|----------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|------------|
| American buttercream | Dense, very sweet, stable at room temp.                                    | Butter, powder sugar, milk            | Easy       |
| Russian buttercream  | Sweet, smooth, caramel notes, stable at room temp.                         | Sweet condensed milk, vanilla, butter | Easy       |
| German buttercream   | Creamy, rich, silky, less sweet.                                           | Pastry cream, butter                  | Difficult  |
| Ermine buttercream   | Smooth, fluffy, less sweet, stable at room temp but fridge due to milk.    | Flour, milk, sugar, butter            | Difficult  |
| French buttercream   | Rich, velvety, custard-like, less sweet, keep in fridge.                   | Egg yolks, hot sugar sirup, butter    | Difficult  |
| Italian meringue     | Smooth, airy, light, fluffy, fairly stable.                                | Egg whites, hot sugar sirup, butter   | Difficult  |
| Swiss meringue       | Smooth, less sweet, delicate, keep in fridge.                              | Egg whites, powder sugar, butter      | Difficult  |

## Recipes

Frostings can be usually flavoured. Just keep the consistency in mind and don't add fat into meringue before whipping. 

### American buttercream
* 125 g butter, room temperature
* 185 g powder sugar, sifted
* 2 tbsp milk

Cream the butter. Add sugar and milk in two batches while beating constantly. Scrape sides of the bowl as needed. Add more milk or sugar to adjust the consistency. 

### Russian buttercream
* 225 g butter, room temperature
* 395 g sweetened condensed milk, room temperature
* powder sugar, for consistency

Cream the butter. Mix in the condensed milk in four batches while beating constantly. Scrape sides of the bowl as needed. Add powder sugar to adjust consistency.
