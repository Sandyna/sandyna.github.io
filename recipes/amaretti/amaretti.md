## Ingredients
* 80g sugar
* 60g almond flour
* 30g armelline - apricot kernels
* 1 egg white (cca 30g)
* 1 tsp honey
* 0.5g or even less ammonium bicarbonate or a bit more of baking powder
* almond flavour???

## Instructions
Oven 150°C to 180°C, to be determined. <br/>
To be tested. You can start with almond flour or with roasted almonds that you grind into fine powder (with powdered sugar?)<br/>
Some people just mix everything together with a mixer and some first whip the egg whites until they have soft peaks and add them after.<br/>
Coat in powdered sugar. <br/>
Bake 20-25min for chewy, 25-30min for crunchy.<br/>
