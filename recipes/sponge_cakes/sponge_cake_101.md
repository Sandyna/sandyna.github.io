Sponge cakes generally start by foaming eggs in some form with sugar, then adding dry ingredients and sometimes fat to the mixture. As you can imagine, there's like 1000 variations, some of which I'll cover here. <br/>

### Notes on ingredients and techniques
Oil vs. butter: <br/>
Oil makes cake smoother, lighter. Butter adds extra flavour and crispiness, but makes the cake denser. <br/>
Whole eggs vs. separate: <br/>
Fluffiness can come from baking powder or from incorporating air in the form of egg whites. Make sure there is no yolks in the whites. There can be some fat, according to [this person](https://www.seriouseats.com/is-it-true-not-to-get-yolk-in-egg-whites), that ran some serious testing. <br/>
Sugar: <br/>
If the recipe needs the sugar dissolved, it's better to use powdered or caster sugar. You can also blend granulated sugar to make it finer. <br/>
Cream of tartar: <br/>
Potassium bitartrate. Weinstein Backpulver. Stabilizes the egg whites, but it's not easily available in Europe. <br/>
Keeping the cake moist: <br/>
For extra moisture, or if the cake is used later, it can be moistened with a simple sugar syrup. Or a flavoured one. Or booze. <br/>
Flour and dry Ingredients: <br/>
Sifting removes clumps and incorporates air. <br/>

### Sponge cake variations

| Name       | Texture              | Difficulty  |
|------------|----------------------|-------------|
| [Genoise](https://bonnibakery.com/genoise-sponge/)    | Very light, springy  | High        |
| [Angel Food](https://bonnibakery.com/angel-food-cake/) | Airy, cloud-like     | Medium      |
| [Joconde](https://bonnibakery.com/opera-cake/#recipe)    | Light and flexible   | Medium      |
| Chiffon    | Moist, tender        | Easy-medium |
| [Hot Milk](/recipes/hot_milk_sponge_cake/hot_milk_sponge_cake.html)   | Velvety, moist, puddingy     | Easy        |


| Name       | Eggs               | Dry ingredients  | Wet ingredients |
|------------|----------------------|-------------|------|
| Genoise    | Bain marie. Dissolve sugar in eggs over double boiler before transfering them to a mixer. | No baking powder. |Butter or oil. |
| Angel Food | Whites whipped with sugar, no yolks. | No baking powder. Sift twice. Super-fine sugar. | Only vanilla, no fats. |
| Joconde    | Whites whipped with part of sugar. Whole eggs in the batter. | No baking powder. | Almond flour added with eggs. Butter. |
| Chiffon    | Whip the whites and yolks separately. | Baking powder | Just properly mix wet with dry, no creaming needed |
| Hot Milk   | Whole eggs.                           | Baking powder | Warm milk + melted butter |
