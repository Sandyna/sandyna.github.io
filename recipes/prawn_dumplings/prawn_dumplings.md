## Ingredients
* 500g Ground shrimps
* 3-4 Spring onions chopped
* 20g Fresh coriander
* 1-2 cloves Garlic, minced
* 1⁄2 tsp Ginger, minced
* 1⁄4 Red chilli
* 1 egg
* 3⁄4 tsp Pepper
* 1⁄2 tsp Salt to taste
* 2 tsp Sesame oil
* 2 Tbsp Light Soy sauce
* 1 Tbsp Cornstarch
* 10 Sheets Gyoza wrappers defrosted

## Instructions
1. Peel and devein the prawns then pulse in a food processor (or chop up with a
knife) until it resembles ground mince.<br/>
2. Mix shrimp, onions and egg together.<br/>
3. Season the remaining ingredients and add the corn starch to help bind the mix.<br/>
4. Mix all the ingredients together and prepare for filling the wrappers.<br/>
5. To form the dumplings, remove 1 gyoza wrapper from the package, covering the
others with a damp cloth. Brush the edges of the wrapper lightly with water.<br/>
Place 1/2 rounded teaspoon of the mixture in the center of the wrapper. Shape
as desired. Set on a sheet pan and cover with a damp cloth. Repeat procedure
until all of the filling is gone.<br/>
6. Place a medium non-stick skillet on stove top, medium heat. Add oil to bottom
of pan. Place dumpling in pan, let the bottom get crispy. Pour about 2
tablespoons water into pan. Place cover on top of pan. Let cook until water
evaporates. Plate and serve with some dipping sauce.<br/>
