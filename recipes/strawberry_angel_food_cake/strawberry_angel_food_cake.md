## Ingredients

### Strawberry reduction
* 450 g Strawberries fresh or frozen
* 100 g Granulated Sugar (caster sugar)
* Elderflower blooms or mint (optional, want to try)
* Lemon zest or juice (optional, want to try)

### Cake batter
* 12 Large Egg Whites (or 12 medium egg whites in the UK) about 400g, room temperature
* 300 g Superfine Sugar (Caster sugar)
* 1 teaspoon Cream of Tartar
* 140 g Cake Flour
* ¼ teaspoon Salt
* 1 teaspoon Vanilla Extract
* 2 drops Pink Gel Food Color (optional)

### Chantalilly cream with strawberries (optional)
* 240 ml Heavy Whipping Cream (or double cream, at least 35% fat)
* 28 g Powdered Sugar (less if desired)
* 1 Vanilla
* 2 cups Fresh Strawberries

## Instructions

### Strawberry reduction
Place the strawberries and 100g sugar in a high-sided saucepan with a splash of water, over medium heat. <br/>
Heat until the strawberries start to break down and begin boiling. Use a wooden spoon or potato masher to help squish them down. Stir regularly. Keep boiling until a lot of the liquid has evaporated and the mixture looks slightly gelatinous, about the thickness of jam (about 10-20 mins). <br/>
Let cool for 10 minutes, then add to a food processor and blitz until completely smooth. <br/>
Pass the strawberry puree through a fine mesh sieve to remove any remaining chunks or seeds. (Use a spoon to help push it through the sieve). Let cool completely. <br/>

### Chantalilly Cream
Sift powdered sugar into cold cream and add vanilla. <br/>
With the whisk attachment fitted, turn the mixer on medium-low speed until it starts to thicken, then gradually turn it up to high speed. <br/>
Whip until you see thick grooves forming in the cream from the whisk. Check frequently until it reaches the desired thickness. Don't overwhip, finish by hand if needed. <br/>

### Angel Food Cake
Preheat oven to 180°C. You will need a tube pan, with a hole in the middle. I want to try this with a bundt cake pan instead.<br/>
Make meringue from egg whites and sugar. Try replacing the cream of tartar with a few teaspoons of lemon juice. Meringue needs to be smooth, all sugar dissolved, medium-soft peaks.<br/>
Gently mix in vanilla, coloring and 180g of the strawberry reduction. Once they're incorporated, mix on high for 1 more minute. <br/>
Mix flour and salt and sift twice. Sift it into the meringue, always putting in 1/3rd of the mixture and folding in.<br/>
Spoon the cake batter into an ungreased tube pan. Smooth it out and then bake for 35-40 minutes. A skewer inserted into the middle of the cake should come out clean and the top should be golden brown and springy to the touch. <br/>
Remove from the oven and immediately turn the pan upside down. If your angel food cake pan has little feet on the bottom, rest it on those. If it doesn't, place the pan upside down over a wire cooling rack. Let it cool completely.<br/>
Once the cake is completely cool, use a spatula or knife to run around the edge of the cake pan to help release it, then give the bottom a few taps and the cake should come out. Be extra patient if you're using a bundt cake tin. <br/>
Serve with chantilly cream, fresh berries, and the leftover strawberry puree.<br/>
