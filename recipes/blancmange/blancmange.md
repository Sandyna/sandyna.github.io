## Ingredients
* 1/2 l milk
* 40g cornstarch
* 30g sugar
* Any type of flavouring you want
* Any type of garnish


## Instructions
Whisk the starch in about 1/3 of milk, creating a slury.<br/>
Heat up the rest of the milk. Pour in the slurry, add the sugar and flavouring while stirring. <br/>
Keep stirring and heating until the mixture thickens. <br/>
It can be poured into molds and cooled completely in the fridge or eaten warm.<br/>
For example: You can add a spoon of cocoa or chopped chocolate and garnish with lady finger biscuits and compote.<br/>
