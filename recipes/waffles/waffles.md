## Ingredients
* 450ml full-fat milk
* 3 large free-range eggs, separated
* 125ml vegetable oil, plus extra for oiling if required
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* 225g plain flour
* 5 g (2 tsp) baking powder
* 38g (3 tbsp) caster sugar
* ¼ tsp fine sea salt
* maple syrup, berries, or other stuff to serve 

## Instructions
Heat the waffle iron according to instructions (some need to be lightly oiled before you start).<br/>
Put the egg whites into a clean bowl and whisk to stiff peaks. Mix the other wet ingredients in a jug. Sift the dry ingredients into a large bowl. Pour the jug of wet ingredients into the bowl of dry ones and whisk together, making sure there are no lumps.<br/>
Slowly fold the egg whites into the batter.<br/>
Cook in waffle iron according to instructions. Serve immediately. <br/>
