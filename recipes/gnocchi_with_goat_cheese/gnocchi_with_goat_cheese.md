## Ingredients
* 1 pack gnocchi (400-500g)
* Butter
* Black pepper, ground
* 1/2 Lime
* 1 sprig mint
* 150g goat cheese
* 1 tsp chives
* 1 tbsp cream cheese, fe. philadelphia

## Instructions
Melt butter in a wide pan, enough to cover the bottom and then some. Put it on medium-high heat and bloom black pepper briefly. Add gnocchi, coat them in the butter, let them fry for a bit, maybe a minute. <br/>
Lower the heat to medium, then cook the gnocchi according to instructions on the package, stirring occasionally (6 minutes). One minute before they are done, add a few leaves of mint, thin slice of lime and a dash of lime juice. Stir and turn the heat up to medium-high/high. Let the gnocchi develop a crust, then take them out of the pan. Remove the mint and lime. <br/>
Mix the goat cheese with a dash of lemon juice, salt, pepper, about 1/2 tsp finely chopped mint leaves, finely chopped chives and cream cheese. Mix and add ingredients gradually, tasting as you go. You can leave out some ingredients entirely, if you so desire. <br/>
Serve the gnocchi with the sauce on side, to dip in. <br/>
