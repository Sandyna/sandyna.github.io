## Ingredients
* 160g Polenta, coarse
* 950g Water or broth
* 50-100g Parmesan, grated
* Butter
* Black pepper, ground
* Nutmeg, ground
* Salt
* Oil

## Instructions
Ratio is 1:4 polenta volume to water. Fe. 1 cup polenta to 4 cups water. Other things are used to taste. <br/>
Bring water with 1 teaspoon of salt to boil. Slowly add polenta while stirring, bring back to boil and cook until it thickens and gets creamy. <br/>
Take off the heat and add the parmesan and a few spoons of butter. Season and salt to taste. <br/>
Pour out on an oiled tray, about 1.5-2cm thick layer. Let cool completely, for 30-60min. Cut into rectangles or use cookie cutters to get shapes. <br/>
Heat up a bit of oil in a pan, medium-high heat. Place the cakes into the pan and let them develop a golden crust. They'll stick to the pan initially, but once the crust is ready, they'll unstick. You should be able to see the crust from the side; gently prod them to see if they are unstuck. Flip and repeat for the other side. Serve. <br/>
