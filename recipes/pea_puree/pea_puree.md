## Ingredients
* 2 shallots
* 250g peas, cooked
* 200ml heavy cream
* lamb's lettuce (poľníček, valeriana locusta) to garnish
* Olive oil
* Salt
* Black pepper, ground
* 1/4 tsp White pepper, ground
* 1/4 tsp Nutmeg
* Balsamic vinegar

## Instructions
Dice shallots, roast on olive oil. Add salt and black pepper, stir, then add the peas and cream. <br/>
Let it come to boil, turn off. Add white pepper and nutmeg. Blend until smooth, taste and season more. <br/>
Serve with the lamb's lettuce seasoned with balsamic vinegar. Good to serve with [scallops](/recipes/scallops/scallops.html).<br/>
