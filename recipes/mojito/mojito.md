## Ingredients
* Mint leaves, 3-4 stalks worth
* 100-200g Brown sugar, just eyeball it
* 2-4 Limes
* Water or sparkly water
* Rum, if desired

## Instructions
Wash the mint and remove about 10-15 leaves. Place in a blender with brown sugar, blend until it looks like wet sand. <br/>
Place the minty sugar, remaining mint leaves, lime juice and a few lime slices in a jug. Stir to reduce volume and to distribute ingredients evenly. Add water just to cover the ingredients. <br/>
Leave in the fridge overnight for extra flavour. Add more water and stir until the sugar dissolves. Strain, dilute to taste, add rum or ice as needed. <br/>
