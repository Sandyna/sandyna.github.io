## Ingredients
* 110g pecans
* 240g flour
* 150g butter
* 40g sugar
* 1 egg yolk

## Instructions
Only little bit sweet, very very crumbling. <br/>
Mix dough, bake at 160C for more than 10min until right. <br/>
