## Ingredients
* Milk
* Chocolate, high quality
* Starch (or Xanthan gum)
* Toppings and flavorings

| Chocolate type  | Chocolate | Milk   | Starch |
|-----------------|-----------|--------|--------|
| Milk chocolate  | 40g       | 60g    | 1.4g   |
| White chocolate | 50g       | 50g    | 1.4g   |
| Dark chocolate  | 35g       | 65g    | 1.4g   |


## Instructions
Chop or blend the chocolate to make it easier to melt. <br/>
Mix starch into the milk. Heat up the milk almost to boil, until it thickens slightly (about 95°C is the activation temperature for starch) <br/>
Take off the heat. Put the chocolate into the hot milk, stirring vigorously until it's properly incorporated. <br/>
Stir in flavorings or garnish with your toppings of choice. Some ideas: Strawberry white, milk chocolate with chopped hazelnuts, dark with almonds and amaretto, white with cacao nibs, dark with raspberry liquor and raspberries. <br/>
Notes: The chocolate is super rich and thick, almost pudding-like in consistency. 100ml is a pretty big portion.<br/>
