## Ingredients
* 6 eggs
* 120g sugar
* 60g flour

## Instructions
Preheat the oven to 175°C <br/>
Separate the eggs. Foam the yolks with sugar, until they're pale, fluffy, and the sugar has dissolved. <br/>
Mix in the flour. Beat egg whites into stiff peaks and fold them into the batter. <br/>
Pour onto a tray lined with baking paper, bake for about 10min, until it start smelling and gets golden brown and a toothpick comes out clean. <br/>
Take the tray out and flip the cake upside-down. Peel off the baking paper and roll it. Let it cool rolled up. <br/>
After it's cool, fill with your preferred filling: Whipped cream (needs to be stiff, so use double cream or a stiffener), berries, mascarpone frosting, jam, curd, chocolate ganache, chocolate spread, buttercream, pastry cream... Anything goes. If you pick a wet filling (such as whipped cream), it's best to "seal off" the cake with jam first. <br/>
