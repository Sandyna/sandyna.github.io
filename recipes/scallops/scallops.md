## Ingredients
* 10 big scallops, cleaned
* Salt
* Black pepper, ground
* Olive oil
* Butter
* 2 cloves of unpeeled garlic
* 2 sprigs thyme
* Lemon

## Instructions
Pat the scallops dry. Really really dry. And then some. Let them sit on paper towels for 10 minutes. <br/>
Season with salt and pepper from both sides (or do the other side in the pan). <br/>
Squish garlic with the flat side of the knife. Prepare two generous cubes of butter and the thyme. <br/>
Get a pan very hot, 7-9. Non-stick pan is the easiest, cast-iron second, steel is the most difficult. <br/>
Add a bit of olive oil into the pan, spread it a bit and wait briefly for it to get hot, then place the scallops into the pan so that they don't touch. <br/>
They will stick initially, but trust the process. Let them develop a crust, then start gently prodding them to see if they are ustuck yet. They need about 2-3 minutes. Be careful not to tear them. Once unstuck, flip them to the other side and reduce the heat to medium. <br/>
Add the butter, garlic and thyme. Tilt the pan and baste (scoop the sauce over) the scallops with the sauce for 1-2min. The butter should be browned. <br/>
Serve immediately with lemon juice to taste. Can be served on a bed of [pea purree.](/recipes/pea_puree/pea_puree.html) <br/>
